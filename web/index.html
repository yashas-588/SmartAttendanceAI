The Process

How It Works

From camera to cloud in under a second.

1

Camera Captures Faces

The live webcam feed captures frames continuously. Up to 4 faces can be detected simultaneously from near or far distances.

2

Anti-Spoofing Verification

Blink detection, motion analysis and texture scoring verify this is a real live person — not a photo or video spoof.

3

DeepFace AI Recognition

The verified face is matched against the student database using FaceNet embeddings with cosine distance similarity.

4

Firebase Cloud Sync

Attendance is instantly saved to Firebase Firestore with timestamp, name, and status — visible across all dashboards in real-time.
